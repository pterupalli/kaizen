          Guide — excelling where you are

          Make your work visible

          Sysadmin work is invisible when it goes well. Keep a running log of what you fixed, automated, or hardened, with rough time saved or risk removed. Bring it to your Operations Director in plain business terms, not ticket counts.

          Sequence your proposals

          You're already doing this right — leading with the license cleanup before the CSP switch. Keep stacking wins in that order: quick, low-risk, measurable first; bigger asks once you've built trust.

          Turn breadth into depth on paper

          You're deliberately building breadth (M365, Exchange, Google Workspace, networking, Odoo, AWS). Certifications are how that breadth becomes legible to an outside recruiter — keep the MS-900 → MS-102 → Network+ → AZ-104 sequence moving even at 30 minutes a day.

          Document as you go

          Every migration, proposal, and fix is future interview material and future Japan-job evidence. Write a short one-paragraph case study for each meaningful project while it's fresh.

          Build outward relationships

          Vendors, external partners (like Fidobe on Odoo), and your Operations Director are your best references later. Treat every cross-team project as a chance to be the person people vouch for.
